One framework for the full evidence lifecycle

Parakh takes on the slow, manual parts of a digital investigation and applies them consistently, so investigators can focus on judgement and decisions.

सं

Evidence collection

संग्रह

Ingest phone extractions, call records, chats, emails, statements and logs into one common structure.
वि

Pattern and anomaly analysis

विश्लेषण

Machine learning highlights unusual timings, repeated contacts and irregular transactions, and states why.
प्र

Integrity and origin

प्रमाणीकरण

Every file receives a digital fingerprint on arrival, so its integrity and source can be demonstrated later.
धो

Fraud and manipulation detection

सह

Cross-source correlation

सहसंबंध

स्वदेशी

Indigenous by design

Most forensic tools are built abroad and assume foreign formats, languages and workflows. Parakh is developed in India, around the evidence Indian investigators actually handle.

Data sovereignty

Deploys on your own infrastructure, so sensitive case data does not have to leave your organisation or the country.

Built for Indian data

Handles Indian telecom records, UPI and bank statement layouts, and chats that mix Hindi, English and other Indian languages.

Explainable findings

Each flag shows the evidence behind it, so conclusions can be questioned, verified and presented with confidence.

किनके लिए

Designed for agencies that investigate

Parakh supports any team that must establish the facts from digital evidence.

Police and cyber cells

Handle seized devices and records with a clear chain of custody.

Forensic laboratories

Standardise how evidence is examined, verified and reported.

Financial investigators

Trace suspicious transactions and link them to communications.

Regulators and auditors

Verify the origin and integrity of records under review.
